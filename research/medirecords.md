# MediRecords, research sheet (second pass, from the vendor's own help centre)

**Method:** public pages only, read in the built-in browser and by fetch: medirecords.com product pages, the Knowledge Base at support.medirecords.com (category pages, "Patient Dashboard", "Appointments Series 1", "Today's Notes" articles and their screenshots). No sign-in, nothing submitted. Independent user reviews are still thin (Capterra: 2 reviews), so praise and complaints remain low-confidence. The design below follows their structure and feature set, not their wording, logo, icons or exact colours.

## Product map (vendor pages)
Care (practice and clinical record: records, ePrescribing, appointments, investigations, billing and claiming, correspondence, assessments), Premium, Connect (FHIR and APIs, BI dashboards, migration), Engage (patient portal and app: bookings, medication history, results, tasks), Evolve (AI suite: Direct document sorting, Patient Summary, Better Billing coming), Admissions, marketplace (Heidi AI scribe, eRx, HealthLink, Stripe, UpToDate, ConsultMed, Snapforms, Coviu telehealth). Cloud, ISO 27001 family, AU-hosted.

## Knowledge Base structure = the real module list
Getting Started; Product Releases; Evolve; Premium; Engage; Connect and FHIR; Billing and Payments; Appointments; Clinical Record; Medications; Communications; Patient Records; Resource Management; Settings and Configuration; Reports and Audits; Admissions; App and Widget.
- **Appointments:** appointment book (Day, Week, Month, Agenda, List), available-times search by provider or user group, double bookings, telehealth, managing, audit history, SMS and email reminders and confirmations (custom SMS linked to appointment types), sessions, **waiting room** (arrive a patient), wait list, appointment types, provider filter.
- **Clinical Record:** Activities (tasks, clinical actions, work lists), Consultations and **Today's Notes** (SOAP, timer, autosave, auto-insertion of clinical data, consult note lock, templated shortcuts, attachments, confidential past consults, expanded clinical view), Heidi AI scribe, immunisations and AIR, medical history, observations and vitals, clinical drawings, allergies and reactions, obstetrics and cervical screening, **requests and results** (secure messaging inbox for results, request templates), My Health Record, clinical templates and enhanced primary care, admission history, procedures and theatre lists.
- **Medications:** writing prescriptions, S8, authority and streamlined, PBS/RPBS, active script list, eRx configuration, real-time prescription monitoring.
- **Communications:** SMS templates and SMS on demand, email on demand, letter templates and mail merge, letter writing (interested parties), correspondence in and out, practice chat, secure messaging (HealthLink, Medical Objects; inbox, outbox, **holding bay**), notice board.
- **Patient Records:** details and identifiers (IHI validation, fund cards, DVA), patient notes in the header, consent, patient views (search by mobile or Medicare, custom patient lists, bulk SMS from the list), **Patient Dashboard** with preventative health alerts (RACGP Red Book).
- **Billing:** claiming dashboard, Medicare, DVA, ECLIPSE, claim errors, assignment of benefit, bulk billing, private invoicing, Stripe payments, refunds and adjustments, accounts (receivable, reconciliation, journal, expenses, deposits), fee schedules.
- **Reports and Audits:** appointment and consultation dashboards, patient reports (demographics, referrals, immunisation, visits, procedures, prescriptions), financial (sales dashboard, provider earnings, banking, deposits, refunds), population health, system, patient and integration audit, invoicing and claiming audit.

## Screen anatomy (from help-article screenshots)
- **Quicklinks bar (top):** product mark, practice or location dropdown, colour-coded create buttons **+Patient, +Appointment, +Clinical Action, +Task**, a "Find a patient" box, mail and calendar icons, user menu.
- **Left sidebar (collapsible):** Home, Appointments, Waiting Room, Patients, Workflows (expand), Accounts (expand), More (Resources, Settings, Reporting, Audit, Utilities).
- **Appointments page:** Today, previous and next, date range, patient search, **Day, Week, Month, Agenda, List** tabs; right side SMS balance, Providers dropdown, Filter dropdown, New Appointment. Provider columns, grey unavailable time, hover summary of the patient's appointments. **Status legend under the calendar: Late, Unavailable, Booked, Confirmed, Waiting Room, With Provider, At Billing, Completed.** Four places to create an appointment (quicklinks, Appointments tab, a calendar slot, the patient Quick Actions bar).
- **Patient record:** header with initials avatar, name, sex, mobile, red alert icons, a pill for activities, Chat and Engage buttons. **Tabs: Dashboard, Details, Clinical, Accounts, Consent, Activities, Audit.** Under them a Quick Action toolbar: SMS on demand, Messages, Invoice (new, new in-patient), Appointment, Clinical Action, Consultation.
- **Dashboard:** Summary and Notes sub-tabs; widget grid (Current Medications, Medical History, Immunisations, Investigations, Pregnancy Details), each showing the latest 3 items and a "View ..." button; last 10 consult notes under Notes; right column with **Preventative Health** (alerts with a plus to create an action) and **Chat History**.
- **Clinical tab:** vertical sub-nav (Today's Notes, Allergies and Reactions, Medical History, Past Consultations, Medications, Immunisations, Cases, Clinical Drawings, Procedures, Admission History, Investigations, Correspondence, Observations, Obstetrics, Cervical Screening). Today's Note editor: consult type, date and time, room, timer; insert row (Reason, Observation, Examination, Procedure, Drawer, Diagnosis, Shortcuts, New EPC, Cases); formatting toolbar; Save. SOAP sections are filled in a fixed order when SOAP is on.
- **Inbox:** Secure Messaging Inbox for Results and for Correspondence: view, filter, search; turn an item into a task or clinical action; reviewed items file into the patient record; unmatched items wait in the Holding Bay; Outbox shows what was sent.

## Praise and complaints
Independent: very little. One Capterra review praises support and copy-paste of medication lists but finds the medication list untidy. Vendor claims: stability, cloud, no servers, 7am to 7pm AEST support.

## Where PMS can be clearer (our angle)
Saved locally, sent, acknowledged and needs-review as separate states on every send; AI draft visibly separate with a review gate; integration status always visible; audit on the patient screen.
