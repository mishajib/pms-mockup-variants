# Halaxy — research sheet

**Confidence:** vendor pages, help centre and prior in-repo screenshot analysis (92 screenshots) = good. Independent reviews: Capterra/GetApp pages returned 403; Trustpilot yields one 1-star (email-delivery/system issues, anecdotal). Live-product study: **pending** (browser pane opened on halaxy.com; waiting for user to sign in; no credentials will be typed).

## Layout and navigation model
Left sidebar: Patients, Schedule (Calendar, Appointment list, Waiting list, Tasks), Finances, Reports, Contacts (incl. Order Results, secure messages), Personal, Settings. Top bar: patient search, inbox, notifications, chat. Patient record tabs: General, Clinical Notes, Clinical Dashboard, Funding, Invoices, Appointments, Social History (from /home/shajib/Documents/PMS/docs/halaxy-analysis.md).

## Hero-screen anatomy (Calendar)
Day/Week/Month, multi-location + practitioner filter, per-day custom hours, calendar preferences (status colour, clinical-note status, payment label, invoice status, fee balance, new-patient icon). **Right panel** for appointment: patients tab (group appts with fees), personal events, repeat, status, Medicare claim + referral, cancel/delete dialog, telehealth link.

## Key features
Core software "100% free", revenue from paid add-ons (payments, rebates, SMS). Online booking + directory, telehealth built in, intake forms, patient portal (notes, referrals, scripts, pathology results), 700+ GP clinical templates/tools, AI Scribe + AI referral/summary, eScript (eRx registration added Sep 2025), Sonic Labs pathology results (alerts Jun 2025), HealthLink / ReferralNet / Medical Objects, Xero, API, recalls/express appts/waiting list/rooms, Medicare/NDIS/private/WC/TAC rebates with auto-updated MBS fees.

## What users praise
Ease, price (free core), online-first, fast setup, 24h support claim, one platform for many professions.

## "Clumsy" points
- Independent: one review notes "older design generation, less intuitive" for some practitioners (Capterra summary via search); Trustpilot single review on reliability/email delivery. Both low-sample.
- Gaps in the vendor GP pages: **AIR and My Health Record are not named** (GP page, 2026 top-10 article). Telehealth only a nav link. Results integration is Sonic-specific.
- Add-on charges (SMS, payments) accumulate (competitor blog).
- Built for many professions: GP depth (results inbox review, recall queue, vaccine status) is thin; clinical record is notes-first.

## Integrations
Medicare, eScript/eRx, Sonic Labs, HealthLink, ReferralNet, Medical Objects, Xero, Stripe-style payments.
