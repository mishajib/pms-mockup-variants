# Xestro — research sheet

**Confidence:** vendor blog/partner pages = ok; independent reviews: none found. xestro.com.au did not resolve (xestro.com is the live domain). Features page not retrieved, so module list is partial.

## Layout and navigation model
Cloud on AWS; web + mobile (my.xestro.com). One platform for patients, appointments, billing, clinical records. Aimed at **specialists and private practices** (not GP-first); 4,500+ specialists / 3,600+ locations (CEO claim). Layout `[unknown]`; no screenshots.

## Hero-screen anatomy
Not documented publicly. Partners reference recalls, templates, billing, reporting, waitlist, scheduling, correspondence.

## Key features
Appointment mgmt, billing, clinical docs, compliance; real-time dashboards (financial/operational, vendor claim); automation (auto-text sign-off, equipment orders from admission bookings, booking confirmation emails); Xestro Hub scanner auto-naming/categorising of referrals/results/correspondence; Tyro EFTPOS integration (reduces keying errors); Medicare and private health fund integration; pathology and secure messaging; DICOM; MIMS integration work; 2-step verification, restricted staff access, suspicious sign-in alerts; migration programme for VIP.net practices (sunset ~June 2027); partner network (RCM consultants, virtual reception, IT).

## Second pass (modules the Xestro variant now follows)
Public pages only; **no screenshots or help centre found, so layouts are our own** and only the module list and workflows come from Xestro.
- Home page (https://xestro.com/): patient communication linked to the record, automated pre-appointment emails or print, dictation or built-in transcription, scheduled financial reports to email, 2-factor and ISO 27001 claim (vendor claim), appointment confirmation SMS with response processing, referral expiry notices, quoting with automatic gap, remote access. Feature areas: Patient Management, Telehealth, Practice Management (multiple entities and revenue centres, export to accounting packages), Obstetrics (antenatal to postnatal). Integrations: Services Australia, eRx, MIMS, HealthLink, Medical Objects, Tyro, Sonic, Healius.
- FAQ (https://xestro.com/frequently-asked-questions/): pregnancy is one admission open for the whole episode (current pregnancy, antenatal visits, postnatal, documents); admissions marked complete; pregnancy audit by type, provider and time frame; obstetrics report from the admission; theatre and delivery lists built from appointment types per provider; recalls and lists built by the practice; multi-merchant payments from one terminal; Medicare, DVA, ECLIPSE, WorkCover Qld; usage-based add-ons; shared seats; no built-in DICOM viewer.
- Training post (https://xestro.com/2025/11/12/onsite-practice-management-training/): Quick Letter, document templates, digital forms (registration on a tablet or QR code), pre-registration linked to automated messages, investigation request forms with signature.

## What the variant adds (nav groups: Patient management, Correspondence, Practice management, Admin)
Admissions (obstetric episode with current pregnancy, antenatal, postnatal, documents; procedure admissions with booking and billing), Theatre and delivery lists, Quotes with gap, Letters and forms (Quick Letter, forms, transcription queue), Automations (rules, previews, replies processed), Reports (revenue by entity and centre from invoices, pregnancy audit, multi-merchant payments, scheduled report), Telehealth (simulated video).
Not copied: wording, colours, logo. Not built: DICOM (Xestro has none), eRx and Medicare screens beyond the shared claims pipeline, VIP.net migration.
